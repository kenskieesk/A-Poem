A Suprise For You

A poem for you

A haze of light, trembling with doubt,
The world blurs, its lines without route.
I grope through shadows, guessing what’s near,
A fleeting figure begins to appear.

And in that moment, strange yet clear,
As though a curtain slowly drew near—
You emerged, shattering hesitation,
Stillness replaced all imagination.

Your face—engraved like the moon’s embrace,
A smile that pierces the heart’s quiet space.
Your eyes, like dawn’s first tender flame,
Chasing the night, calling my name.
Before you, storms learn to cease,
A fragile hope becomes my peace.

The world once dim, blurred and cold,
Fades before the beauty you unfold.
No longer illusion, no fleeting disguise,
But an anchor cast in empty skies.

Amid the clamor of life unsure,
You stand—a harbor, steadfast, pure.

And when the night changes, the stars softly bend,
Yet love, unyielding, knows no end.
Through seasons that fade, through years anew,
My soul still journeys, ever to you.

Perhaps this is love, quiet and true,
For even my weary eyes
See poetry in you.
